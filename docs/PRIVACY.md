# FlowForge mobile privacy policy

Effective November 5, 2026. This policy describes the native mobile application in this repository, not the independently hosted Streamlit website.

FlowForge mobile does not require an account. The application code does not send learner predictions, explanations, simulation results or notebook history to a server. It includes no advertising or analytics SDK. Synthetic simulation inputs and results are generated on the device.

Experiment history, including explanations typed by the learner, is saved locally using device application storage. Do not enter private information into an experiment explanation. Uninstalling the app normally removes its local application data; operating-system backup and restore behavior depends on device settings.

When you choose **Share my experiment notebook**, the app gives the notebook to the operating system's share interface. The recipient or service you select handles the shared information under its own policies. Sharing is optional.

Apple, Google, Expo and the operating system may process information needed for distribution, crash diagnostics or platform operation according to their own policies. This policy does not claim control over those services.

Questions or privacy concerns can be raised through the project's [support issue tracker](https://github.com/jabuniaj/flowforge-simulator/issues). Do not include sensitive information in a public issue.

The owner must review this policy against the final signed application's dependencies and store privacy disclosures before submission. Future networking, accounts or analytics would require an updated policy and appropriate disclosures.
