**Name:** FlowForge: Shift Challenges

**Subtitle:** Learn operations by doing

**Description:**

A warehouse shift is falling behind. Can you fix it with the people and budget you have?

FlowForge turns operations concepts into small, playable engineering challenges. Move workers between picking, packing and shipping. Decide when an extra workstation helps—and when the real problem is equipment downtime. Predict the effect, run ten simulated shifts, and watch the queues build or recover.

- Three missions with different constraints.
- Local simulations that work without a connection.
- Queue playback and explanations tied to your decisions.
- An experiment notebook saved on your device.
- Optional notebook sharing.

Designed for students exploring industrial engineering, supply chain, manufacturing and process improvement. No account is required.

All missions use synthetic assumptions for learning. Results are not forecasts for a real warehouse.
